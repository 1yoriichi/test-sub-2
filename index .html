Our Story

Fire is our first ingredient.

Founded by chef Aanya Rao, Spice & Ember reimagines the great regional kitchens of India through the lens of live-fire cooking.

Every dish begins at the hearth — charcoal embers, a glowing tandoor, and spices toasted to the edge of smoke. We source heritage grains, line-caught seafood and single-estate spices, then plate them with the precision of a modern tasting room.

The result is food that feels both ancient and entirely new: bold, layered, and unmistakably ours.

Spice is memory. Fire is transformation. We cook at the place where the two meet.
Aanya RaoFounder & Executive Chef
